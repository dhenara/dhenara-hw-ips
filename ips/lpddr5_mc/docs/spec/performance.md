# Performance and fairness acceptance

The reference demonstrates a feasible periodic schedule for the agreed default combined AXI payload target. It does not measure controller RTL. `performance.witness` emits a fixed schedule; `performance.check` independently maintains CA, bank, split-DQ, maintenance and host-resource state. `tools/qualify.py` binds that schedule to its exact numeric profile and retains results.

## Exact benchmark

Use two 128-bit AXI ports at 400 MHz, two independent x16 channels at CK/DFI=800 MHz/WCK=3200 MHz, one 8 Gb rank per channel, BG BL32, 64-byte aligned full bursts, read/write DBI and both ECC modes off, always-on WCK during traffic, 64 command/response credits per channel per direction and three CK request/return CDC allowance. Both ports are saturated, their eligible work balanced across channels and sixteen banks. Keep responses ready; all W data must arrive before a write is eligible. Use successive rows rather than an infinite row-hit assumption. The example integration descriptor has capacity beyond the addressed workload and no aliasing.

The high-service mode enables selection every two CK (CK every cycle is also checked). The reduced mode enables every four CK. Pair completion and legality remain CK-rate. Commands are one CK; actual selections have at least the one-CK feedback margin. The host ledger reserves a pick one CK before DFI issue; DFI issue precedes the witness's DRAM pin command by the explicit two-CK PHY path. It retains that reservation through issue. No hidden slower-clock feedback or unbounded descriptor preload is assumed.

A period is 3000 CK (3.75 µs). At its start REFab occupies 168 CK; sixteen adjacent ACT pairs then open different banks, constrained by RRD4/FAW16/RCD15. Each bank/row receives at most 29 full bursts. The high-service witness initially interleaves two BGs at offsets zero/two of each eight-CK group, then issues every eight CK, for 375 bursts/channel/period. The reduced witness uses 339 bursts with CK/4 selection. Every BL32 occupies sixteen beats, a sixteen-half-WCK gap, then sixteen more beats; pin collisions are checked interval by interval.

After host data drains, WS_OFF/full disable precede background ZQ latch at CK2928 (tZQLAT24). PREab at2954 closes all banks. Fresh standalone synchronization at2956 permits MR4 at2960; its BL16 opcode data reserves the four-CK effective MRR window, outside host payload. WS_OFF2984 and disable complete before the next REF. Precharge recovery, read/write final pin recovery, CA collisions, WCK ownership and all periodic obligations are checked across period boundaries. Mandatory RFM counters remain enabled: this low-ACT workload is discharged by each periodic REF, never reaches the maximum and therefore issues no RFM. Separate stress tests force RFM at the bound. Thermal code nine (or cooler under the conservative no-extension policy) is the benchmark condition; hotter operation follows the separate refresh/derating contract.

Two x16 channels have 25.6 GB/s raw capacity at 6400. The first row above consumes half that raw capacity and includes row/maintenance overhead in the schedule. Two 256-bit AXI400 ports already equal the raw memory peak, leaving no protocol margin. Higher widths remain legal interfaces with backpressure; this benchmark makes no same-clock raw-peak sustained promise.

## Measurement and buffering

Model host arrivals occur after three CK of CDC, one 64-byte request every eight CK per channel at full rate. Reduced arrivals are backpressured to the measured rate. An initial twenty-entry backlog is reproduced after every period, rather than consumed as a finite preload. Read words enter the response ledger only after the final physical split half, the two-CK PHY deserializer path and the three-CK return crossing. Read response service consumes four AXI beats over eight CK; its slot remains occupied through the last handshake six CK after the first. B uses a conservative one-per-eight-CK service bound. Both channel ledgers are symmetric; the combined rate is their sum in one direction.

Run at least four complete periods, discard one warm-up period and measure completed AXI payload over the following integral periods. Report occupancy, live outstanding granules, response slots including the in-service burst, steady completed burst counts, feedback/CDC assumptions, exact clocks/configuration and commands. No tail drain is included in the steady window. The checked reference requires identical periodic backlog, exactly 375/339 completions in every measured period and no capacity above64. `qualify.py` retains the actual maxima.

For future combined RTL, run at least 100 periods, discard the first ten, and require completed payload ≥99% of the reference rate over the following 90 integral periods, no overflow/lost/duplicate data and a bounded steady backlog. The 1% tolerance accommodates deterministic start/CDC alignment and must be reported as missing bytes/stall causes; unexplained continuing growth fails regardless of average rate. Compare emitted legal commands to the independent checker. Measure reads and writes separately; do not add their peaks. Mixed/random, narrow/partial, ECC, backpressure, hot thermal, rank switches and maintenance stress are separate correctness/performance reports without a new hidden same-rate promise.

For the fixed combined-RTL write benchmark, the implemented steady-backlog check uses the last complete warm-up REF interval to freeze a service-ready floor (complete W awaiting B). Every complete measured REF interval must return to that floor or below, with all AW identities from the preceding recurrence retired, including incomplete-W reservations. Complete sampled source/clock and REF coverage is mandatory; missing source evidence, nonadjacent calibration, omitted interior intervals or an overdue trailing REF reject the check. Saturated AW/W offer is observed directly, while legitimate WREADY backpressure remains permitted. The original AW/no-B endpoint comparison remains reported as a diagnostic. Read gating is unchanged.

A selected sufficient guard for live carry-in and partial measurement edges bounds AW-to-B by three admitted maximum REF intervals (9372 CK for the normal 3124-CK, no-postponement profile). The scale follows full transaction turnover across arbitrarily phased consecutive recurrence cuts. Its extension to edge cohorts is specific to this fixed benchmark, not a universal latency or fairness promise. The [verification guide](../verification/system.md) defines admission, accounting and retained-evidence analysis. Payload, protocol/data, capacity and exact settlement remain independent required checks; tail responses earn no measured bytes. A passing finite trace does not prove indefinite stability or qualify the separate read direction.

## Scheduling, caps and fairness

Age is a saturating 16-bit count of `AGE_TICK_DIV` CK ticks; the qualified default is one age increment every eight CK. Service enables never change age units. Oldest urgent traffic takes priority over row hits, but never bypasses legality or strict caps. Empty-direction escape is unconditional even below batch minima. A stale/duplicate pick must replay or fault without consuming another credit.

RD_CAP/WR_CAP are bytes per fixed BW_WINDOW CK, charged at actual issue. The cap oracle accepts an explicit byte amount; a 32-byte request charges 32. Strict mode cannot overshoot at the window edge or due to pipelined picks. Work-conserving mode borrows unused capacity only when the opposite direction has no eligible work. Zero strict capacity intentionally disables that direction and suspends its progress promise; it is not interpreted as unlimited. BW_MODE 0=strict,1=work-conserving. Positive read/write weights define weighted round-robin opportunities inside a batch; zero disables that weighted share. Urgent oldest traffic can override weights but cannot override a zero strict cap. QoS maps 16 AxQOS values into four classes; within each class admission age/tag breaks ties. All classes must receive the same urgent-age escape.

A fairness window contains continuously eligible traffic only: no older overlap hazard, exhausted strict cap, missing W, response backpressure, unavailable ECC credits or required ownership/maintenance pause. With queue depth D, threshold A ticks, tick divider T and a guaranteed issue opportunity at least every G eligible CK, the conservative oldest-first bound is `(A+1)*T + D*G`. At D=64/A=32/T=8/G=64 this is 4360 eligible CK. This is conditional on a bounded live issue gap, not a promise under arbitrary PHY or host stalls. Measure suspended time separately. `check_fairness` checks every grant gap and the final wait through observed_until; a grant early in the trace cannot hide a starved tail. Later RTL must establish G for each claimed workload and test both ports/classes, rather than infer fairness from throughput alone.
