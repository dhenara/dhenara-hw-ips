## Engineering findings

**Throughput and backlog.** All six 100-period measurements meet the unchanged 99% criterion over the specified 90 measured periods, without tail-drain credit. Reads deliver 12.8 GB/s in CK, CK/2 and CK/4 selections. Writes deliver 12.7926044, 12.7935526 and 12.7918459 GB/s respectively. The references remain 12.8 GB/s for CK/CK2 and 11.5712 GB/s for CK4; full-service write deficits are 2,496 and 2,176 bytes.

Write/CK4's measured endpoint backlog is 50 to 62 per channel, so the equal-endpoint predicate remains false. That does not imply accumulated unfinished service here: all three write selections return completed-W/no-B occupancy to the independently established warm-up floor of six during every one of 90 complete REF intervals on each channel. Every prior outstanding AW retires before the next recurrence, with no slow identity. Maximum CK4 AW-to-B age is 1.0975 microseconds, while occupancy never exceeds 64 credits. The checker validates continuous sampled clocks, saturated AW/W offers, actual handshakes, REF coverage, data/pin prerequisites and complete settlement. This supports bounded steady operation for the measured workload; it is not an indefinite-time or equal-endpoint claim. Stall categories overlap, so they are not a one-to-one attribution of each missing byte.

**Integrity and recovery.** The final code retains one serialized protected-granule owner through physical data/check commit and correction scrub. This satisfies the shared-check-line, lock and stale-scrub safety invariants without a multi-entry dirty cache. Owning scrub-versus-rewrite, poison/full-rewrite, partial failure and combined host/ECC tests cover that implementation. The contract permits posted success at or after physical completion; the composed implementation conservatively waits for final integrity. Standalone epoch tests exercise early exposure and late-error mechanics; no composed early-posting performance is claimed.

The ECC diagnostic correction requires an unsaturated retained stale-counter baseline, no new stale completion after settlement, retained late-error history and healthy fresh service. Requiring zero would incorrectly erase historical cancellation diagnostics. The MR43 boundary correction still requires actual returned data, recovery and WCK completion. Reset dispatch omits cost measurement only for its deliberately idle prefix and continues to require final physical recovery/cost checks; the independent tests include rejection of corrupt, empty or unclosed recovery. Matrix reset observations bracket reset, complemented by the separate continuous fault/reset trace. Confirmed destructive reset never proves data preservation.

**Protocol and management.** Current APB/coordinator reproduction, dirty update/master cases, FSP decline/timeout and fresh natural-RFM/active-PHY-refresh cases cover the changed publication/rearm owners. Earlier datapath-only evidence is reused only where those changed owners are absent or their instantiated source is unchanged. Two-rank power dispatch is limited to the actual broadcast power commands and checks each rank's timing/state. Ordinary two-rank power and owning FSP evidence do not imply every two-rank fault combination.

**Coverage and delivery.** The joint behavior review maps all six procedures to owning and combined evidence: AXI parameters/order/errors/overlap/custody, pin/command/slice identity, ECC arithmetic/raw transport/composition, management/recovery, caps/fairness/performance and reproducibility. Arithmetic coverage enumerates all single and unordered double positions for the selected codewords/payloads; raw transport doubles are selected. Thirteen cap and eighteen fairness selections retain complete tails and sensitivity checks. Fairness remains conditional on the documented measured G values; the earlier row-write G64 failure remains failed. There is no Cartesian-matrix or successful trailing-W ownership-entry claim. Current docs, generated sources and programming examples are covered by final checks. Prior pending/failure reports remain historical and are not rewritten.
